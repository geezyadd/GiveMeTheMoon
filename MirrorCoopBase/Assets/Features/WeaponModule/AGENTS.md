# WeaponModule — weapons

The weapon core, its one network path and the client feel. Read the [root AGENTS.md](../../../../AGENTS.md) first (game, rules, module map). Change this module's description here, not in the root file.

## Assembly
- `Features.WeaponModule.Contracts` (layer 1; references: NetworkModel): `IWeaponTarget` (only `IsAlive`; what a hit does is a role on top: `IWeaponDamageTarget.ServerReceiveHit` for rocks and the debug dummy, `IWeaponPullTarget` for ore), `WeaponHit`, `IWeaponTargetCollider`, `IWeaponTargetRegistry`, `IWeaponTargetSource`, `IWeaponImpactFeedback`, `IWeaponPullTarget` + `WeaponPull` (a target a rope can hook and pull, field ore), `IWeaponHolderAnchors` + `WeaponHolderAnchor` (every peer: the weapon a player holds in the hand now and its muzzle, by `PlayerKey`); a weapon target such as a field rock implements them without referencing the weapon module; separate from `GameCoreModule.Contracts` so that one stays free of NetworkModel / Mirror.
- `Features.WeaponModule` (layer 2; references: WeaponModule.Contracts, CameraModule.Contracts, NetworkModel, Zenject, Mirror): the weapon core, its one network path `WeaponHolderSync` and the client feel, see "Weapons" in Key places below; it does not reference the ship, the flight field or the player; its inspector part picker is `Features.WeaponModule.Editor`. The minigun turret on the ship is [TurretModule](../TurretModule/AGENTS.md).

## Key places
| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Weapons | `Assets/Features/WeaponModule/`. A weapon is one `WeaponDefinition` asset (`GameResources/Weapons/`: stable id, name, shots per second, damage per hit, range, the parts, a `WeaponFeelProfile` with the look numbers: muzzle flash, casings, recoil, camera shake, tracer every N, impact) in `WeaponCatalog_Default` (`Configurations` group, `WeaponCatalogValidator` at startup). Parts are `[SerializeReference]` objects inside the definition, picked in the inspector from a type menu (`WeaponPartPickerAttribute`): fire mode (`IWeaponFireModeDefinition` -> runtime `IWeaponFireMode`; `AutoFireMode` with barrel spin-up / spin-down), shot delivery (`IShotDeliveryDefinition` -> `IShotDelivery`, outcome through `IShotOutcomeReceiver`, may come later than the shot; `HitscanDelivery` with a spread cone over an injected `IWeaponRaycast`; `PhysicsWeaponRaycast` takes the nearest solid collider or trigger that carries an `IWeaponTargetCollider`, so a trigger with no target, such as a field structure's volume, lets the shot through, and carries a struck `IWeaponImpactFeedback` in the hit and the outcome, which `WeaponShooter` shows on every peer that fires or replays the shot before passing the outcome on), hit effects (`IHitEffect` list; `DamageHitEffect`: damages an `IWeaponDamageTarget`, any other target is not damaged; `HookAndPullHitEffect`: hooks an `IWeaponPullTarget` with its `WeaponPull` (pull speed, rope length), any other target finds no hold); `RopeDelivery` (`RopeDeliveryDefinition`: a ray as thick as its catch radius along the aim, no spread, through `IShotDeliveryContext.CreateSphereCast`, `PhysicsWeaponRaycast` with a radius); holder and aim (`IWeaponHolder`, `IWeaponAim`) and the client feel (`IWeaponFeel`) are interfaces only. The server checks a reported hit with `WeaponHitRule` (pure: weapon held, target alive, range + tolerance, fire rate budget `WeaponShotBudget` with tolerance and burst from `WeaponHitRuleConfiguration_Default`); targets implement `IWeaponTarget` from `Features.WeaponModule.Contracts`. A new weapon is a new asset and, if needed, one new part type, never a `switch` on a weapon type (`WeaponCatalogGuardTests`). A delivery definition builds its runtime part from an `IShotDeliveryContext` (`PhysicsShotDeliveryContext`: `PhysicsWeaponRaycast` on the delivery's `_hitLayers`, triggers included; the minigun hits only layer `FieldRock`, never the own ship or players; a collider of a target carries an `IWeaponTargetCollider` with its id); `IWeaponShooterFactory` gives every holder its own `WeaponShooter` (fire mode + delivery), whose outcomes go to `ReportedShotOutcomes` on the holding peer (feel + hit report) or `ShownShotOutcomes` on every other peer (feel only). **Network path, one for every weapon:** `WeaponHolderSync` on the player prefab: the owner sends trigger + aim direction in the holder's own frame (`CmdSetAim`, the holder throttles it; other peers read the SyncVars and replay the fire themselves, no message per bullet) and its target hits through `WeaponHitQueue` in fixed-slot `WeaponHitBatch`es (4 hits, no allocation) at most once per network tick (`CmdReportHits`); the server keeps a `ServerWeaponHolding` (weapon, catalog index, shooter key, server-side aim, fresh `WeaponShotBudget`) set by the holder (`ServerBeginHolding` / `ServerEndHolding`), and `WeaponHitService` checks each hit with `WeaponHitRule` and applies the weapon's hit effects to the target from `IWeaponTargetRegistry` (Contracts; targets register by id on the server, `WeaponTargetRegistry`, or a whole family registers once as an `IWeaponTargetSource` that answers for its own id range, as the field rocks do). **Feel** (`Feel/`, client only, everything pooled at `Bind`, 0 B GC per frame of continuous fire): `MinigunFeel` (the slot view drives it with `Advance`) with `BarrelSpin`, `MuzzleFlash`, `RecoilJitter`, the camera shake through `ICameraShakeService` (the seated owner's view only: a continuous fire shake while it looks through the gun and a one-shot shake from impacts within `NearImpactDistanceMeters`; profiles `CameraShake_MinigunFire` / `CameraShake_MinigunNearImpact` in `GameResources/Weapons`, referenced by the `WeaponFeelProfile`), `CasingStream` (casings simulated in the floor's frame, `IWeaponFeelFloor`: they ride with the ship, bounce and pile on the deck and are never physics bodies), `TracerStream`, `ImpactBursts` (flash, sparks, chips, dust in the frame's custom simulation space). The muzzle flash (`MuzzleFlash`) is one small glow and a short light **per shot** (not per frame of fire), kept small, dim and short so the gunner sees the stream of tracers past it (2026-10-27, was a glow every frame of fire; size / light / seconds was -> now: light gun 0.8 / 5 / 0.06 -> 0.28 / 1.5 / 0.03, minigun 1.2 / 8 / 0.05 -> 0.35 / 2 / 0.03, heavy gun 2 / 14 / 0.08 -> 0.45 / 2.5 / 0.035, pistol 0.35 / 6 / 0.06 -> 0.14 / 1.5 / 0.035; colour alpha 1 -> 0.7; tracers unchanged). The weapons have no sound (removed on 2026-10-27 with `WeaponFeelAudio` and the clips). Every number is in `MinigunFeelProfile`. **Debug:** the debug window's Weapons → "Spawn / Remove the weapon test dummy" on the host (editor / development builds, `WeaponDebugTargetSystem` / `IWeaponDebugTargets`, the spawned dummy in `WeaponDebugTargetModel`) puts a `WeaponDebugTarget` dummy in front of the ship (layer `FieldRock`; it sways from side to side, counts and flashes the hits the server accepted). Everything about it is compiled only in editor / development builds: its prefab and ship offset come from `WeaponDebugTargetConfiguration_Default` (`Configurations` group, bound under the same define), and `WeaponDebugTargetPrefabSystem` registers the prefab with the Mirror client on every client start, so it is not in the network manager's spawn prefabs. Who shoots is only the server's `IWeaponShooterIdentity` (`ServerWeaponHolding`); a peer's `IWeaponHolder` gives just the trigger and the aim. |

## Tiers, the pistol and their parts
- Weapons in `WeaponCatalog_Default`: `Minigun`, `LightMachineGun`, `HeavyGun` (the turret tiers, see [TurretModule](../TurretModule/AGENTS.md)), `Pistol` and `Harpoon` (see [HandWeaponModule](../HandWeaponModule/AGENTS.md)).
- A feel that shows the held trigger (barrel spin, fire shake: `MinigunFeel`) also implements `IContinuousFireFeel.PresentFiring`; `WeaponShooter` calls it only on such a feel, `HandGunFeel` and `RopeFeel` show only their shots.
- `RopeFeel` is the feel presenter of a rope weapon in the hand (the rope line out and back through `RopeCast`, recoil kick, view kick); `WeaponFeelProfile` got a Rope block (`RopeOutSpeedMetersPerSecond`, `RopeReturnSpeedMetersPerSecond`, `RopeHoldSeconds`).
- `WeaponHolderAnchorRegistry` (`IWeaponHolderAnchors`, `IWeaponHolderAnchorRegistry`, bound in `WeaponModuleInstaller`): the hand weapons held on this peer by player key; `HandWeaponWielder` registers while it holds one.
- `SingleShotFireMode` (`SingleShotFireModeDefinition`): one shot per press, cooldown = one shot interval of the weapon's shots per second; readiness 0 -> 1 over the cooldown.
- `WeaponFeelProfile` got `RecoilKickMeters` / `RecoilKickDegrees` (a single shot's kick, `RecoilKick`), `ShotShake` (the holder's view kick per shot of a single-shot weapon). `HandGunFeel` is the feel presenter of a gun in the hand (a muzzle flash per shot, kick, tracer, impact, view kick).
- `IWeaponCatalogIndexService` (`WeaponCatalogIndexService`) gives a weapon's catalog index **by id**: in a player build a scene spawn prefab holds its own copy of a definition, apart from the Addressables catalog's (a reference match threw `KeyNotFoundException` on the coop client). The turret station uses it too.
- `HeldWeaponReadinessModel` (bound in `DataInstaller`, local): the local player's weapon in hand for the HUD ring around the crosshair (`HeldWeaponReadinessView` in `GameHudWindow`, LobbyModule).

## Tests
- `SingleShotFireModeTests`, `RopeCastTests` (the rope flies out at its speed, holds on a caught target, a missed one is reeled in and gone), `WeaponCatalogIndexServiceTests` (a copy of a definition finds its index, an unknown one throws).
- `Features.WeaponModule.Editor.Tests` (auto fire cadence and spin-up / spin-down at any frame rate, hitscan spread bounds and outcomes, every `WeaponHitRule` branch and the fire rate budget, weapon catalog guard, `WeaponTargetRegistryTests`: target sources)
- EditMode guard: `WeaponCatalogGuardTests` (weapon ids set and unique, every weapon has all its parts, the startup validator passes and fails on a shared id, no weapon code branches on a concrete weapon).
- In the weapon tests `WeaponHitQueueTests`, `WeaponHitServiceTests` (the server hit path) and `WeaponShooterTests` (only the holding peer reports hits).
