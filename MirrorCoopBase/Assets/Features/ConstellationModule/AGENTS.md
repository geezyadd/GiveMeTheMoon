# ConstellationModule — constellations highlighted on the sky

A constellation drawn over the painted stars of the skybox: each star like a bright star photographed through a telescope (a small bright core, a soft halo, four thin diffraction spikes) and glowing lines of its figure. A test
for now: only Orion, always on, only in `LobbyScene`. Read the [root AGENTS.md](../../../../AGENTS.md) first (game,
rules, module map). Change this module's description here, not in the root file.

## Assembly
- `Features.ConstellationModule` (layer 1; no references): the constellation data, the sky frame and look
  configurations, the mesh builder, the view. No Zenject: the view runs in edit mode too, where nothing is injected.
  No tests yet.

## Key places
| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Constellation as data | `ConstellationDefinition` (`GameResources/Constellations/Orion.asset`): display name, stars (name, RA and Dec in degrees, J2000, V magnitude) and the lines of the usual figure as pairs of star indices. A new constellation is a new asset, no code. Orion: Betelgeuse, Bellatrix, Meissa, Mintaka, Alnilam, Alnitak, Saiph, Rigel; lines Meissa-Betelgeuse, Meissa-Bellatrix, Betelgeuse-Alnitak, Bellatrix-Mintaka, the belt, Alnitak-Saiph, Mintaka-Rigel. Positions and magnitudes from AstroTracker's `ObjectCatalog/stars.txt` (Rigel 0.18, Betelgeuse 0.45, Bellatrix 1.64, Alnilam 1.69, Alnitak 1.74, Saiph 2.07, Mintaka 2.25, Meissa 3.39). |
| Where the painted sky lies | `CelestialSkyFrameConfiguration` (`GameResources/Configurations/CelestialSkyFrameConfiguration_LobbySky.asset`, for `StarSkybox04.mat`, `_Rotation` 0): a catalogue position (RA a, Dec d) is written in Unity's axis order (x = cos d cos a, y = sin d, z = cos d sin a: the swap that turns the right-handed celestial frame into Unity's left-handed one), then turned by `Euler(0, -90, 0)`. So RA 0 / Dec 0 is world +Z, RA 90 is -X, the north pole is +Y: a camera with yaw = -RA and pitch = -Dec looks at (RA, Dec); the celestial equator is the lobby's horizon. Found on 2026-11-27 by plate solving 1024 px, 60 deg renders of the skybox with AstroTracker's solver (8 frames, 38-51 matched stars each, fit RMS 26" = 0.11 px); Betelgeuse, Rigel, Bellatrix and Sirius land within 0.1 px of the painted stars. Another skybox, or a skybox `_Rotation`, needs its own frame. |
| Look | `ConstellationHighlightConfiguration_Default`, all sizes in degrees for a star of the reference magnitude (1.0). Magnitude: light = 10^(-0.4 (m - 1)); size factor = light^0.5 and brightness factor = light^0.5, each clamped to 0.35..2 (Rigel x1.46, Betelgeuse x1.29, Bellatrix x0.85, belt x0.56..0.73, Meissa x0.35). Star, three layers in one quad, tint (0.95, 0.97, 1): core = Gaussian, radius 0.04, intensity 8 (HDR, so the scene's Bloom, threshold 1, gives a tight glow); halo = (1 - r / 0.55)^3, intensity 0.3; spikes = a cross of four, length 1.4, width 0.015 at the centre, intensity 1.6, width and brightness taper to zero at the tip (narrower than a pixel they keep a pixel's width and dim instead), turned 15 deg clockwise from an upright '+'; twinkle = the halo breathes by 8 % at 0.7 rad/s, a phase per star (0 turns it off). Line: a strip 8 px wide on screen, tint (0.45, 0.62, 1): a thin Gaussian core (intensity 1.3, a little above Bloom's threshold) over a soft glow (0.18) filling the strip, fading in over 0.25 deg at each end; it stops 0.3 deg x the star's size factor short of each star, so the belt keeps its lines. |
| Drawing | `ConstellationHighlightView` (`[ExecuteAlways]`, on the `ConstellationHighlight` object in `LobbyScene`, serialized refs to the three assets, its `MeshFilter` and `MeshRenderer`) builds the mesh with `ConstellationMeshBuilder` on enable and hands the star / line settings to the shader per submesh (property blocks; `_IsLine` picks the look). Every vertex is a world direction: a star is a quad of four directions around it, its axes the spike axes (screen right / up for a camera looking at the star with world up on top, turned by the spike angle), UV0 = shape position in degrees, brightness, twinkle phase; a line is a quad of its two end directions, UV1 = the other end's direction, and the vertex shader widens it sideways in pixels (a great circle projects to a straight line, so the strip stays on the stars). `ConstellationAtInfinity.shader` (`GameResources/Shaders`, material `GameResources/Materials/ConstellationHighlight.mat`) projects a direction with the view-projection matrix and w = 0, so the camera's position is ignored like the skybox's, and puts the depth just inside the far plane: the figure never slides against the stars when the player walks or flies, any world geometry hides it, it is unlit, unfogged, added onto the sky (transparent queue, `Blend One One`). The mesh has huge bounds so it is never frustum-culled. Local only: nothing is networked. |

## Known limits
- Only `LobbyScene` has the highlight; `GameScene` uses the same skybox and frame but has no highlight object yet.
- The stars are drawn over the painted stars, not from them: the size follows the catalogue magnitude, but the colour is one tint for all (Betelgeuse looks orange only through its painted star).
- The spikes follow world up, not the screen: when the camera rolls (or looks near a pole) they turn with the sky.
- The view reads the configuration on enable: a change in the asset shows after the object is re-enabled.
- Checked in editor renders (batchmode camera renders of `LobbyScene`), not in Play mode.
