# HandWeaponModule — weapons carried in the hand

The pistol and every later weapon a player holds in the hand like a tool. Read the [root AGENTS.md](../../../../AGENTS.md) first (game, rules, module map). Change this module's description here, not in the root file. The weapon core and its one network path are [WeaponModule](../WeaponModule/AGENTS.md).

## Assembly
`Features.HandWeaponModule` (layer 4; references: Grab, Camera, CharacterMovable, Input, InputRealization, NetworkModel, WeaponModule, Mirror, Zenject). No installer: `HandWeaponWielder` sits on the player prefab and is injected with it.

## Key places
| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Hand weapons | A hand weapon is one `HandWeaponDefinition` (`GameResources/PistolHandWeapon`: id, name, icon, description, price, item prefab, the `WeaponDefinition` it fires, `MinTriggerReportSeconds`, `CrosshairLayers`), sold by the shop after the tools (`ShopCatalog.HandWeapons`). The item (`GameResources/PistolItem`: primitives, `Grabbable`, `NetworkIdentity`, `HandWeaponItem`, the feel `HandGunFeel` from WeaponModule, `InteractionTooltip` with `Tooltip_Pistol`, `ZenAutoInjecter` for the feel's camera shake) is picked up, held and dropped like the wrench, and is in the network manager's spawn prefabs. `HandWeaponItem` (an `IWeapon`; `HandDefinition` is its hand weapon, `Definition` the weapon it fires) binds the feel once, in a feel frame of its own in the item's scene (`OpenAirFeelFloor`), so a tracer does not swing with the hand. **`HandWeaponWielder`** (player prefab, every peer) watches the hand (`GrabController.Held`): while it holds a hand weapon item it equips the item on the player's `WeaponHolderSync` (`Equip`, every peer; the item is an `IWeapon`, its magazine a `WeaponMagazine` on the item), so the local player's `WeaponSystem` fires it (LMB) and reloads it (R); on the owner's peer the shot leaves the muzzle toward the point under the crosshair (`CrosshairAim`: the view's centre ray on `CrosshairLayers`, through the holder's own body) and the aim goes to the holder (`OwnerSetAim`); every other peer turns the model along the synced aim (`HandWeaponHolder`). Letting go, dropping or losing the item ends all of it (`Unequip`). The old trigger latch and owner input are gone: every shot now goes to the server and to every peer as its own message (WeaponModule, "The weapon system"). |

## The pistol
150 in the shop; `Pistol` weapon: one shot per press (`SingleShotFireMode`, cooldown = 1 / 3 shots per second = 0.33 s since 2026-11-26, was 2 s; a press within the cooldown is lost, holding never fires again), 8 rounds, R reloads in 1.5 s, hitscan on FieldRock, PlayerRagdoll, Default and Ground (was FieldRock only), 12 damage (breaks any small rock: at most 12 HP; pops every balloon: at most 3 HP), 150 m, a struck player is pushed 8 m/s. The other hand weapons (knockdown gun, grenade and rocket launchers) are in WeaponModule's "Hand weapons now". Feel (`PistolFeelProfile`): a small muzzle flash per shot, recoil kick (5 cm, 9°), tracer every shot, impact burst, view kick `CameraShake_PistolShot` for the holder; no sound.

## The pistol in the hand
The item's model (`RecoilRoot`, with the muzzle: `HandWeaponItem._poseRoot`) is placed while held, every frame, by `HandWeaponPoseDriver` (from `HandWeaponWielder`; the pure math is `HandWeaponPose`): in its holder's own **first-person** view (`GameCameraModel.ActiveId` = FP camera, no fixed view) it sits at `HandWeaponDefinition.ViewOffset` in the view's frame (pistol: right 0.17, down 0.16, forward 0.42 m), placed in `IGameCameraService.LookApplied` right after the look, so it turns with the view on the same frame; otherwise (the holder in third person, every other peer) it sits at `HandOffset` from the arm point (pistol: right 0.55, down 0.05, back 0.15 m: out at the body's right side, so it shows past the capsule) and points along the holder's aim (the owner's crosshair aim, the synced aim on other peers; an empty synced aim keeps it along the arm). Let go, the model is back on the item (`ResetPose`). The shot leaves the muzzle where the model is shown.

## Known limits
- A hand weapon placed in a scene (the lobby rack) must not carry `ZenAutoInjecter`: the scene context injects it, and
  the injector's exception stops the injection of the whole scene. The prefabs keep it for items spawned at runtime.
- There is no arm or hand model: the pistol floats beside the capsule body. From right behind (the own third-person camera) it is seen end-on and small.
- A rejoining player's hand is empty (the item drops on leaving), as for every item.
