# HandWeaponModule — weapons carried in the hand

The pistol, the harpoon and every later weapon a player holds in the hand like a tool. Read the [root AGENTS.md](../../../../AGENTS.md) first (game, rules, module map). Change this module's description here, not in the root file. The weapon core and its one network path are [WeaponModule](../WeaponModule/AGENTS.md).

## Assembly
`Features.HandWeaponModule` (layer 4; references: Grab, Camera, CharacterMovable, Input, InputRealization, NetworkModel, WeaponModule, Mirror, Zenject). No installer: `HandWeaponWielder` sits on the player prefab and is injected with it.

## Key places
| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Hand weapons | A hand weapon is one `HandWeaponDefinition` (`GameResources/PistolHandWeapon`: id, name, icon, description, price, item prefab, the `WeaponDefinition` it fires, `MinTriggerReportSeconds`, `CrosshairLayers`), sold by the shop after the tools (`ShopCatalog.HandWeapons`). The item (`GameResources/PistolItem`: primitives, `Grabbable`, `NetworkIdentity`, `HandWeaponItem`, the feel `HandGunFeel` from WeaponModule, `InteractionTooltip` with `Tooltip_Pistol`, `ZenAutoInjecter` for the feel's camera shake) is picked up, held and dropped like the wrench, and is in the network manager's spawn prefabs. `HandWeaponItem` binds the feel once, in a feel frame of its own in the item's scene (`OpenAirFeelFloor`), so a tracer does not swing with the hand. **`HandWeaponWielder`** (player prefab, every peer) watches the hand (`GrabController.Held`): while it holds a hand weapon item, on the server the player holds that weapon (`WeaponHolderSync.ServerBeginHolding`, so the hit reports are checked as for the turret); on the owner's peer the `Fire` input (LMB, `HandWeaponOwnerInput`; off while a window frees the cursor or the controls are blocked) is the trigger, `HandWeaponTriggerLatch` keeps a click reported at least `MinTriggerReportSeconds` (0.25 s) so every peer sees it, the shot leaves the muzzle toward the point under the crosshair (`CrosshairAim`: the view's centre ray on `CrosshairLayers`), the aim is sent in the player's own frame with the trigger (`OwnerSendAim`, only when the trigger changes) and the HUD ring shows the cooldown (`HeldWeaponReadinessModel`); every other peer replays the shot from the synced trigger and aim (`HandWeaponHolder`). On every peer the wielder registers its player's weapon as an anchor (`IWeaponHolderAnchorRegistry`, WeaponModule: the player's `PlayerKey` from `GrabController.Key`, the weapon id, the live muzzle), so a rope is drawn from it and the server breaks a rope whose holder let go. Letting go, dropping or losing the item ends all of it. |

## The pistol
150 in the shop; `Pistol` weapon: one shot per press (`SingleShotFireMode`, cooldown = 1 / 0.5 shots per second = 2 s; a press within the cooldown is lost, holding never fires again), hitscan on `FieldRock`, 12 damage (breaks any small rock: at most 12 HP), 150 m. Feel (`PistolFeelProfile`): a small muzzle flash per shot, recoil kick (5 cm, 9°), tracer every shot, impact burst, view kick `CameraShake_PistolShot` for the holder; no sound.

## The harpoon
120 in the shop (`GameResources/HarpoonHandWeapon`, item `HarpoonItem`: primitives, the same parts as the pistol item, `RopeFeel` instead of `HandGunFeel`, `InteractionTooltip` with `Tooltip_Harpoon`, in the network manager's spawn prefabs in `GlobalScene`). `Harpoon` weapon (`WeaponModule/GameResources/Weapons/Harpoon`): one rope per press (`SingleShotFireMode`, 1 / 0.667 shots per second = 1.5 s to reel in), a rope delivery (`RopeDeliveryDefinition`: straight along the aim, catches what passes within 1.5 m of the line on `FieldRock`), 70 m, 0 damage, one hit effect `HookAndPullHitEffect` (pull 22 m/s, the rope breaks beyond 80 m). LMB throws the rope at the point under the crosshair: on every peer `RopeFeel` draws it (a plain `LineRenderer`) flying out to where the shot ended and reeling back (`RopeCast`: out 140 m/s, a caught target holds it 0.35 s, back 110 m/s; `HarpoonFeelProfile`); a hit on a piece of ore goes the one weapon path to the server, which hooks it ([FlightFieldModule](../FlightFieldModule/AGENTS.md), "Ore"): the piece is pulled to the ship at the pull speed and every peer draws its rope from the hooker's muzzle until the piece reaches the ship (money) or the rope breaks (the holder lets go, takes another item, goes beyond the rope). A miss or a rock in the way hooks nothing. The player may walk while pulling. No sound.

## The pistol in the hand
The item's model (`RecoilRoot`, with the muzzle: `HandWeaponItem._poseRoot`) is placed while held, every frame, by `HandWeaponPoseDriver` (from `HandWeaponWielder`; the pure math is `HandWeaponPose`): in its holder's own **first-person** view (`GameCameraModel.ActiveId` = FP camera, no fixed view) it sits at `HandWeaponDefinition.ViewOffset` in the view's frame (pistol: right 0.17, down 0.16, forward 0.42 m), placed in `IGameCameraService.LookApplied` right after the look, so it turns with the view on the same frame; otherwise (the holder in third person, every other peer) it sits at `HandOffset` from the arm point (pistol: right 0.55, down 0.05, back 0.15 m: out at the body's right side, so it shows past the capsule) and points along the holder's aim (the owner's crosshair aim, the synced aim on other peers; an empty synced aim keeps it along the arm). Let go, the model is back on the item (`ResetPose`). The shot leaves the muzzle where the model is shown.

## Known limits
- There is no arm or hand model: the pistol and the harpoon float beside the capsule body. The harpoon has the pistol's hand offset and its own view offset (right 0.18, down 0.17, forward 0.38 m). From right behind (the own third-person camera) it is seen end-on and small.
- A rejoining player's hand is empty (the item drops on leaving), as for every item.
