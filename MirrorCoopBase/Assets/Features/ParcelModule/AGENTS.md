# ParcelModule — delivery parcels

A voluntary risk for more money: a station's "Delivery" stand offers 0-2 parcels for the next station. A parcel is a
crate on the deck that pays a lot when the ship lands at its destination with it on board, but while it is on board the
bugs come more often, grab it and fly off with it; the crew chases the thief, shoots it down and pulls the drifting
crate back with the harpoon. Read the [root AGENTS.md](../../../../AGENTS.md) first. Change this module's description
here.

## Assembly
- `Features.ParcelModule.Data` (layer 1; references: NetworkModel, Mirror, Zenject): `ParcelOffer`, `ParcelEntry`,
  `ParcelState`, the generated Shared `Parcels` model (definition `NetworkModelModule/GameResources/Definitions/Parcels.asset`:
  `Offers`, `Entries`), its writer `IParcelsWriter` and `ParcelsBindingModel`.
- `Features.ParcelModule` (layer 7, above ShopModule; references: ParcelModule.Data, GameCoreModule.Contracts,
  FieldAgentModule, ShipModule, GrabModule, ShopModule (the crew wallet), WeaponModule.Contracts, SonarModule.Contracts,
  DebugModule.Contracts, Game.Connection, NetworkModel, Mirror, Mirror.Components, TextMeshPro, Zenject). It does not
  reference BugModule: the bugs see a parcel only as a FieldAgentModule cargo. Installed by `ParcelModuleInstaller` in
  `GlobalSceneInstaller`; `ParcelsModelInstaller`, `ParcelsBindingModel` and `ParcelServerModel` in `DataInstaller`;
  `ParcelConfiguration` in `ConfigurationInstaller` (checked at startup by `ParcelConfigurationValidator`). Tests:
  `Features.ParcelModule.Editor.Tests`.

| The stand | Two slots, each `Stand/ParcelOfferInteractable` (E with a free hand while `ParcelStandService.IsOffering`: Build and the ship within 80 m; free) and `Stand/ParcelOfferView` (a crate on the slot, "PARCEL +reward to the next station [E] take", the slot's aim trigger only for an open offer so it never hides the taken crate). Taking marks the offer taken and spawns the crate on the slot (`ParcelCrateService`) |

The thief is slower than the ship (BugModule's carry speed) |
| Recovery | The harpoon: `Systems/ParcelWeaponTargetSystem` registers one `IWeaponTargetSource` for the parcel id range, `Server/ParcelPullTarget` is the `IWeaponPullTarget`; `ParcelRecoveryService` hooks a drifting crate at a hit on it, pulls it toward its hooker's weapon at the rope's speed (the rope breaks as the ore's does: holder let go, other weapon, beyond the rope; then it drifts again) and boards it within 2.5 m of the holder or over the deck. | Loss | `Rules/ParcelLossRule` (pure, `ParcelFlightSystem` every frame): carried farther than 250 m from the ship (measured at the thief) or longer than 75 s, drifting farther than 250 m or longer than 60 s, a loose crate 30 m below the deck (overboard, also one left behind on a pad), never while on a rope. ## Known limits
- The stand's offers are random (0-2 per station); a station may offer none.
- A crate drifting in the field does not follow a world shift (there is none in flight; the landing recenter comes after the parcels are settled).
- The crate has no tooltip and no sound.
