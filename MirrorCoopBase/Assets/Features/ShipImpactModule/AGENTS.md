# ShipImpactModule — rock and balloon collisions

The hull's collisions with field rocks and balloons, the knockback feedback, the impact shake and effects. Read the [root AGENTS.md](../../../../AGENTS.md) first (game, rules, module map). Change this module's description here, not in the root file.

## Assembly
`Features.ShipImpactModule` (layer 5; references: Ship, CameraModule.Contracts, DebugModule.Contracts, EffectPool, FlightFieldModule.Contracts, GameCoreModule.Contracts, NetworkModel, Mirror, Zenject): the rock and balloon collisions of the hull, the knockback feedback, the impact shake and effects. Bindings: `ShipImpactModuleInstaller`. The ship core is [ShipModule](../ShipModule/AGENTS.md).

## Key places
| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Rock collisions | `ShipImpactModule/Scripts/` (assembly `Features.ShipImpactModule`, bindings in `ShipImpactModuleInstaller`; its assets stay in `ShipModule/GameResources/`). `ShipHullVolume` (ship core, `ShipModule/Scripts/Hull/`) on `ShipPlatform` (root): boxes in ship space around the deck and the modules (gizmo when selected; data only, no collider). Server, every physics step of `Takeoff` / `Cruise` / `Landing` (not `Build` / `Wreck`): `ShipRockCollisionSystem` (`IFixedTickable`) sweeps the hull boxes from the last checked pose to the pose now (`ShipHullSweep`; a jump longer than `MaxSweepMeters`, a world shift or a debug move, is not swept) and `ShipHullRockScanner` asks `IFieldRockQuery` once per step with the box around all of them (field data, no allocation once the lists have grown; about 0.3-0.4 ms per step in a field), then tests every box. `ShipRockImpactClassifier`: a rock is a small hit when the field says `Small` and breakable, else large (giants are always large). A rock is touched at its kind's share of the data sphere (`IShipRockContactRule` / `ShipRockContactRule`: small `SmallContactRadiusShare` 0.6, large `LargeContactRadiusShare` 0.65, was 1 / 0.8 before 2026-10-26): measured over the rock meshes, the visible surface lies at about 0.53 of the data radius (0.35-0.71 for most rocks and sides), so the whole sphere hit small rocks the hull visibly passed by with metres to spare. Small: `IFieldRockBreaker.ServerBreak` (cause `ShipCollision`, no instigator); only when it broke, `ShipRockDamageRule` damage (linear in diameter 10-35 m from 3 to 12 HP at the reference speed 14 m/s, times speed / 14 clamped to 0.25-2) goes through `IShipHull.ServerApplyDamage` (armor there) and a light push. Large: counted from ahead of the hull along its motion (`ShipRockKnockbackRule.CalculateLargeLookAhead`: 0.8 of the braking distance at the acceleration limit, at most 16 m, + 1 m margin) against its contact share of the rock's data radius; the first contact knocks the ship away from the rock's middle (5 m/s, horizontal) and back against the heading (1.3 x its forward speed), `LargeRockDamage` (0) if set; while the contact lasts the ship keeps moving out at least 3 m/s; the same rock hits again only after the contact ended + `LargeHitCooldownSeconds` (`ShipRockContactCooldowns`). `ShipKnockback` (pure C# in the ship core, owned by `ShipFlight`, which only adds its step to the path it follows, so the ship stays where it was pushed and the pad does not move): the target velocity fades as 1 - t^2 over `LargeKnockbackSeconds` (1.4 s), the velocity follows it with at most `LargeMaxAcceleration` (22 m/s2; the takeoff already starts at about 27 m/s2: 350 m forward and 100 m up in 9 s, so the deck feels nothing new; riders are bound kinematically to the deck, so the acceleration only shapes the feel, it cannot throw a player off), the cruise speed comes back as it fades. Poses reach clients through `ShipPoseSync` as before. Feedback on every peer: `ShipImpactSync` (on the `PoseSync` object; it adds itself to the `ComponentRegistry<ShipImpactSync>`, through which the collision system reaches it) sends `RpcImpact(kind, point, strength)` -> `IShipImpactEvents.OnImpact` -> `ShipImpactCameraShakeSystem` (a local player who rides the ship gets `ICameraShakeService.AddShake` with the kind's profile and the hit's strength; profiles `ShipModule/GameResources/CameraShakes/CameraShake_RockImpactSmall` / `Large`) and `ShipImpactEffectSystem` (pooled `ShipImpactEffect`, `GameResources/Effects/ShipImpactEffect.prefab`: dust and chunks). All numbers in `ShipRockImpactConfiguration_Default` (Addressables, `ConfigurationInstaller`, checked by `ShipRockImpactConfigurationValidator`; per kind `ShipRockImpactFeel`: shake profile and effect size). Debug: the debug window's Rocks → "Aim at the nearest small / large rock" on the host in flight put the ship 40 m before the contact sphere of the nearest small / large rock at its height and turn it there (`ShipImpactModule/Scripts/Debug/`: `ShipRockImpactDebugSystem`, `IShipRockImpactDebug`, also `DebugMoveShipTo`); Rocks → "Show impact volume: off / on" (any player, local; `ShipImpactVolumeDebugSystem` + `ShipImpactVolumeDebugDrawer`, GL lines after every game camera) draws the hull boxes (green) and, for the rocks within 150 m, the contact sphere (small orange, large red) inside the data sphere's horizontal circle (grey) |

## Known limits
- **Rock collisions use spheres.** The server knows a rock only as a sphere from the field data (the turned mesh fits
  inside it with room to spare), so the hull touches a rock at its kind's share of that sphere (small 0.6, large
  0.65): on one side the ship may still touch a little before the visible rock, on another a corner that sticks out
  touches nothing. The hull is boxes (`ShipHullVolume`), not the deck colliders: since 2026-10-26 they follow the
  visible deck and the engines (stern box half width 5.5 -> 4.7 m, bow box 2.5 -> 2.1 m; it was up to 1.4 m wider per
  side than the deck). A client sees a hit through the ship's pose (interpolated like any flight
  motion) and the impact RPC; the two-player test does not check rock hits yet.
- The autopilot keeps its heading after a large hit, so a crew that does not steer bounces off the same rock again
  after the cooldown, and in cruise the ship takes hits from the rocks on its way: the tests' short flights stay in the
  empty station zones.
